# Research Questions & Hypotheses
RQ1: Which customer characteristics are associated with churn?
RQ2: Is product adoption associated with churn?
RQ3: Do NPS and support activity differ between churned and retained customers?
RQ4: Do churn rates differ across plans and acquisition channels?
RQ5: Can customer characteristics predict churn probability?
RQ6: Which signup cohorts show stronger retention?

H1: Product adoption is associated with churn.
H2: NPS differs between churned and retained customers.
H3: Support activity differs between churned and retained customers.
H4: Churn rates differ across acquisition channels.
H5: Customer behaviour and subscription characteristics provide information for predicting churn.
